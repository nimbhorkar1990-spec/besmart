Section G: Granular SEO Automation Permissions
Turn ON/OFF specific automatic SEO operations performed by the AI engine.
Master Switch: Enable Automatic SEO Operations
When OFF, all automatic optimizations require manual approval.
1. Automatic SEO Meta Titles & Descriptions
Automatically generates 50-60 character titles and 120-160 character descriptions.
2. Automatic Relevant Keywords Suggestions & Tagging
Extracts primary and secondary LSI keywords directly from content text.
3. Automatic Internal Linking Integration
Links blog posts to relevant jobs, properties, and search catalog queries.
4. Automatic Image Alt Text Generation
Formulates accessible, keyword-rich alt tags for cover and embedded images.
5. Automatic Schema Markup (Structured Data) Generation
Injects Article Schema.org JSON-LD to empower Google rich snippets.
6. Automatic Sitemap Updates
Appends newly published URLs into search engine sitemap.xml.
7. Automatic Content Quality & Readability Analysis
Computes Flesch Reading Ease scores and highlights thin or low-quality content.
8. Automatic Search Engine Indexing Submissions (Google & Bing)
Submits verified URLs directly to Google Indexing & Bing Webmaster APIs.
9. Automatic SEO Performance Monitoring
Monitors SERP position changes, click trends, and impressions data daily.
